Chat apps no longer get stuck being recorded as a game.

## Fixed
- **Clips:** an app that went fullscreen once (a photo opened in a chat app) stayed a "game" until it was closed; now it stops being one when its window is small again for a few seconds. Alt-tabbing out of a game changes nothing.
- **Clips:** Messy, Messenger, WhatsApp and Telegram are never treated as games.

## Improved
- **Clips:** the recorder log now notes which game was detected and why, when it ended, and what was on screen when recording stalled.
